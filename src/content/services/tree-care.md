faq:
  - question: "Do I need permission before a tree is removed?"
    answer: "Possibly. Protected trees, conservation areas, hedgerow rules and planning conditions can all apply, and they vary by site. Check with your local authority before work is booked — we will tell you if something looks like it needs consent, but the permission itself rests with the landowner."
  - question: "Can you work on a site with restricted access?"
    answer: "Usually, yes — that is a large part of what the assessment is for. What matters is what can physically reach the tree and what the ground will carry. Where a machine cannot get in, the tree comes down in sections instead."
  - question: "What happens to the timber and brash?"
    answer: "That is agreed at quote stage rather than left to the end of the job, because it changes both the work and the price. Chipping, removal or leaving material on site are all options depending on what suits you."
  - question: "Can you deal with a tree that has come down in a storm?"
    answer: "Yes. Storm-damaged and hazardous trees are part of the service. Tell us what has happened and how the site is accessed, and we will advise on what the work involves."
